DISCLAIMER, DATA OWNERSHIP, AUTHORIZED ACCESS & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AGREEMENT

Effective Date: September 5, 2026
Provider: Lottus Services Inc.
Platform: CAL 1.5 – Canadian Accounting Platform

1. Purpose of CAL 1.5

CAL 1.5 is a business accounting technology platform developed and provided by Lottus Services Inc. (“Lottus”) to assist customers in organizing accounting information generated through their business operations, including invoices, payments, expenses, accounts receivable, accounts payable, sales taxes, banking information, journal entries, financial records and accountant-ready reports.

CAL 1.5 is a technology platform and does not replace the professional judgment of a licensed accountant, tax professional, lawyer or other regulated professional.

2. Customer Ownership and Custody of Data

All accounting, financial, operational, customer, supplier, contractor, document and other information entered into, generated by, imported into or connected to CAL 1.5 remains the property of the Customer.

CAL 1.5 is designed using a client-isolated infrastructure model. Lottus does not operate a shared centralized accounting database containing the accounting records of unrelated CAL customers.

Each Customer is intended to maintain its own database, file storage, backup environment and associated cloud infrastructure under an account owned or controlled by the Customer. The Customer retains primary custody and control of its business and accounting data.

3. Authorization Granted to Lottus

The Customer expressly authorizes Lottus, its authorized employees and approved technical contractors, when reasonably necessary, to:

  • create the Customer's CAL 1.5 environment;
  • create or configure the Customer's database;
  • configure cloud infrastructure and private file storage;
  • configure security rules and permissions;
  • connect CAL 1.5 with the Customer's existing business portal;
  • configure integrations;
  • migrate or import authorized Customer information;
  • perform software updates;
  • troubleshoot technical problems;
  • perform system maintenance;
  • investigate reported errors;
  • assist with backup or restoration procedures; and
  • temporarily access Customer systems when reasonably required to provide authorized technical support.

Such access is provided solely for legitimate implementation, maintenance, security, support and platform-operation purposes. Lottus obtains no ownership interest in Customer Data by receiving this authorization. Where practical, administrative credentials and ownership of third-party infrastructure will remain with or be transferred to the Customer.

4. Customer Responsibility for Information

The Customer is responsible for:

  • the accuracy and completeness of information entered into CAL 1.5;
  • determining which persons are authorized to access the platform;
  • maintaining appropriate internal controls;
  • protecting usernames, passwords and authentication devices;
  • immediately removing access for persons who are no longer authorized;
  • ensuring that information entered into CAL 1.5 has been lawfully obtained;
  • obtaining required consents from employees, customers, contractors or other individuals;
  • complying with applicable privacy, tax, corporate, employment and record-retention requirements;
  • reviewing financial information and reports before relying upon them; and
  • providing its accountant or tax professional with accurate and complete information.

The Customer is responsible for activities performed through accounts and credentials issued to its authorized users, except to the extent caused by a security failure for which Lottus is legally responsible.

5. Information That Should Not Be Stored in CAL

Unless specifically authorized and technically supported, CAL 1.5 is not intended to store:

  • online banking passwords;
  • complete payment-card information;
  • credit-card security codes;
  • Social Insurance Numbers unless legally required and specifically supported;
  • medical or clinical records;
  • health-treatment notes;
  • authentication secrets;
  • unrelated highly sensitive personal information; or
  • information that is not reasonably required for legitimate accounting or business purposes.

The Customer agrees not to use CAL 1.5 as a general repository for unnecessary sensitive personal information.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

CAL 1.5 may operate using independent third-party infrastructure providers, which may include cloud database, hosting, storage, email, payment, banking-data, domain, cybersecurity and other technology providers. These services operate independently from Lottus.

The Customer acknowledges that third-party systems may experience outages, scheduled maintenance, unscheduled maintenance, software failures, network failures, service degradation, API failures, regional cloud outages, data-centre interruptions, changes to third-party services, cyberattacks or discontinuation of services.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Lottus will not be responsible for failures, interruptions, loss of availability or other events caused by third-party providers that are outside Lottus' reasonable control.

7. No Guarantee of Uninterrupted Service

Technology services cannot be guaranteed to operate continuously or without interruption. CAL 1.5 is therefore provided subject to normal technological risks.

Lottus does not warrant that CAL 1.5 will be continuously available, uninterrupted, completely error-free or immune from all cybersecurity threats. Temporary suspension or interruption may occur because of maintenance, upgrades, internet failures, infrastructure failures, cybersecurity events or other technical circumstances.

8. Cybersecurity and External Events

The Customer acknowledges that no internet-connected information system can be guaranteed to be completely immune from unauthorized access or malicious activity.

Potential events include, without limitation: hacking; phishing; credential theft; malware; ransomware; distributed denial-of-service attacks; zero-day vulnerabilities; malicious third-party activity; compromised Customer devices; compromised Customer credentials; telecommunications failures; cloud-provider incidents; and other cybersecurity events beyond reasonable control.

Lottus will use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for systems and information within its custody or control. However, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Lottus shall not be liable for losses resulting from external cybersecurity events or circumstances outside its reasonable control. Nothing in this Agreement excludes any obligation that applicable law does not permit Lottus to exclude.

9. Backups and Business Continuity

The Customer acknowledges that maintaining independent and recoverable backups is an important component of responsible information management. Lottus may configure automated backup systems as part of implementation or support services.

However, unless a separate written managed-backup agreement expressly provides otherwise, the Customer remains responsible for confirming that backups are enabled; maintaining appropriate backup retention; maintaining access to its cloud accounts; periodically confirming that important records can be restored; maintaining independent copies of critical financial information where appropriate; and maintaining an appropriate business continuity plan.

Configuration of a backup system does not constitute a guarantee that every file or record can be recovered following every possible event.

10. Accounting and Tax Disclaimer

CAL 1.5 assists with the preparation and organization of accounting information. It does not provide independent professional accounting, audit, tax or legal advice.

Reports generated through CAL 1.5 may include Profit & Loss Statements, Balance Sheets, Trial Balances, General Ledgers, Cash Flow information, Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, GST/HST/QST working information, transaction summaries and other accountant-ready records.

These reports are generated from information available to the system and rules configured for the Customer. The Customer and its professional accountant remain responsible for reviewing the information before filing tax returns, making tax remittances, issuing financial statements or making material financial decisions.

Unless separately agreed in writing, CAL 1.5 does not file tax returns with the Canada Revenue Agency, Revenu Québec or another tax authority.

11. Automated Processing

CAL 1.5 may automatically categorize transactions, generate accounting entries, calculate taxes, associate business operations with accounting records and prepare reports.

Automated processing reduces manual work but cannot eliminate the possibility of incorrect source data, incorrect categorization, unusual transactions or circumstances requiring professional judgment. The Customer is responsible for reviewing material financial information or arranging for its accountant or bookkeeper to do so.

12. Privacy and Lawful Use

Each party shall comply with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements concerning personal information that is within its custody or control.

The Customer is responsible for determining whether it has authority to collect and process the information it places into CAL 1.5. Lottus will use Customer information accessed while providing CAL services only for authorized implementation, maintenance, support, security and related service purposes, except where otherwise required by law.

13. Privacy and Security Incidents

If Lottus becomes aware of a confirmed security or privacy incident involving Customer Data within systems under Lottus' custody or control, Lottus will take reasonable measures appropriate to the circumstances and will cooperate with the Customer in addressing the incident.

The Customer remains responsible for regulatory notifications concerning information under its custody or control except where applicable law imposes a direct obligation on Lottus. The parties agree to reasonably cooperate in investigating material security incidents.

14. Customer-Controlled Infrastructure

Where the Customer's database, storage, hosting or other infrastructure is maintained through accounts owned or controlled by the Customer:

  • the Customer controls continued access to those accounts;
  • the Customer is responsible for maintaining required third-party subscriptions;
  • the Customer may revoke Lottus' technical access;
  • the Customer retains its data if the relationship with Lottus ends; and
  • the Customer may request assistance exporting its information in a reasonably available format.

Lottus does not claim ownership over the Customer's accounting database.

Lottus may temporarily suspend CAL 1.5 access when reasonably necessary to respond to a security threat; protect Customer information; perform emergency maintenance; prevent unlawful use; prevent damage to infrastructure; address a material breach of this Agreement; or respond to a third-party service suspension.

The Customer may discontinue CAL 1.5 subject to its applicable subscription agreement. Because Customer Data is intended to remain within Customer-controlled infrastructure, termination of CAL services does not transfer ownership of Customer Data to Lottus.
