STEP 19.5 · ADMINISTRATOR MANUAL

Operate the whole service lifecycle without rewriting history.

Use the correct operational record at each stage: Request before assignment, Job after conversion, Invoice after completion. Administration may correct future work freely, but completed execution evidence and issued accounting records remain protected.

1Request / Customer2Schedule & assign3Operate / replace before start4Complete & review invoice5Payment / review
01

Dashboard

Start with ACTION REQUIRED. Work new requests, pending Provider responses, schedule changes and invoices ready to issue.

Invoices Ready to Issue

Open the DRAFT invoice queue, review the final customer amount and use SEND INVOICE.

02

Unassigned Requests

Before a Request becomes a Job, use Service Requests or Service Maintenance to change service type, preferred date/time, hours, address, description and internal notes.

Assignment

When READY TO ASSIGN, send it to Master Calendar and build the Provider team.

03

Assigned Jobs

Once a Job exists, the Job is the operational record. Use Service Maintenance to edit service type, date/time, duration, address, Customer Rate and Provider Cost before completion.

Service Type safety

If changing Service Type would leave an active Provider unauthorized for that service, PLEASE blocks the change until the Provider is replaced.

04

Replace a Provider

From Jobs → View → Assignment History choose CHANGE PROVIDER for a PENDING or CONFIRMED Provider who has not started.

Protected replacement

PLEASE removes only that Provider, preserves history, then opens Master Calendar in Correct & Reassign mode. Execution evidence or Provider Payments block removal.

05

Add a Follow-up Service

From Customers or a Job choose + NEW SERVICE / ADD SERVICE FOR CUSTOMER. PLEASE prefills the customer and address into Master Calendar.

Separate Job

A new Cleaning service after Moving becomes a new related Job for the same customer; the completed Moving Job is not rewritten.

06

Started Services

After START, avoid changing who performed the work. Schedule changes are restricted and the original start time cannot be moved once execution has begun.

More time

Use the controlled extension process rather than altering historical start evidence.

07

Completed Jobs

Completed execution is historical. Do not change Provider identity, photos or schedule after completion.

Final customer value

Open the DRAFT invoice and review items/rates before SEND INVOICE. After issue, use accounting corrections instead of rewriting the invoice.

08

Customers

Customer Master keeps reusable contact/address data and previous Requests/Jobs. Use + NEW SERVICE FOR THIS CUSTOMER for repeat work without asking the customer to re-enter their information.

Returning customer

Review prior service context, then schedule the new service independently.

09

Provider Portal Rules

Providers confirm/decline, manage availability, propose schedule changes and execute their own evidence lifecycle.

Declined / Cancelled

These assignments remain in Administration audit history but disappear from the Provider Portal.

10

Invoices & Payment

Completed Jobs prepare DRAFT invoices. Administration reviews final value, then SEND INVOICE. Card payment and e-Transfer remain linked to the Invoice.

e-Transfer

Keep pending until the money is actually confirmed received.

11

Finance / CAL

Daily users record business facts only. GL accounts, debits/credits, Journals, GIFI and compliance stay in Advanced Accounting.

Do not guess

Unknown classifications become Finance Exceptions.

12

Audit Rule

PLEASE favors correction with history over deletion. When replacing Providers or changing active work, reasons and status history are retained.

Boundary

Future operations are editable; completed evidence and issued financial records are protected.
