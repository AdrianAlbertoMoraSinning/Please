AUTOMATION

Completed Job → Invoice

Job completion creates a durable event before the worker runs. Amounts come from frozen Job billing; duplicate invoices are blocked.

Accounting boundary: DRAFT invoices do not post to the ledger. When an invoice is issued, the existing STEP 17 event engine—not this page—creates the Journal/Ledger posting.
