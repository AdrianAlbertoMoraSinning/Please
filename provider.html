PROVIDER USER MANUAL

How to use your PLEASE Provider Portal

A practical step-by-step guide for managing assignments, schedule, evidence, rates, documents and profile information.

PLEASE PROVIDER WORKFLOW

From assignment to completed service

Follow the service lifecycle in order. Your actions create the operational history visible to PLEASE Administration and, where applicable, the customer.

1Review2Confirm3Arrive + Photo4Start5Complete + Photo
01

Overview

Use Overview as your home screen. Check Pending, Confirmed and Completed counters and review your next assignments.

Good practice

Refresh the portal before starting your workday and whenever Administration tells you a Job has changed.

02

My Calendar

Your calendar displays only your own assignments. Use Previous, Today and Next to navigate months and select an event to see Job details.

Privacy

You cannot see another Provider's calendar, assignments or compensation from this view.

03

Assignments

Open Assignments to respond to new work. Review date, time, address, service scope, PLEASE notes and your compensation before accepting.

Required action

Confirm or decline promptly so PLEASE can keep the customer schedule accurate.

04

Schedule Changes

If the assigned time must change, use Propose Schedule Change. Choose date, start and end in 15-minute increments and add a clear reason.

Important

The original schedule remains official until PLEASE Administration approves the request.

05

I've Arrived

When you reach the service location, choose I'VE ARRIVED and take the required arrival photo. On mobile, use TAKE PHOTO to open the camera.

Evidence rule

The official arrival photo must correspond to your own assignment. Do not reuse another Provider's photo.

06

Start Service

After arrival is recorded, start the service. This changes your assignment lifecycle to in progress and updates the operational history.

Sequence

CONFIRMED → ARRIVED → IN PROGRESS. Do not skip lifecycle steps.

07

Request More Time

If additional service time is needed, request an extension from the assignment. Select the hourly item, additional minutes and reason.

Approval

Extra time is not final until the required approval workflow is completed by PLEASE.

08

Complete Service

When your work is finished, select COMPLETE SERVICE and take the required completion photo. The portal records your individual completion.

Multi-Provider Jobs

Every Provider assigned to the same Job must independently complete arrival, start and completion procedures.

09

Availability & Hours

Maintain your normal weekly working hours and date-specific exceptions. Use only :00, :15, :30 or :45 times.

Keep it current

Accurate availability helps PLEASE avoid scheduling conflicts.

10

My Service Rates

Maintain the amounts you charge PLEASE for authorized services. These are Provider compensation rates, not customer selling prices.

Financial separation

PLEASE controls customer prices separately. Existing Job financial snapshots do not change when you edit future rates.

11

Documents & Service Photos

Upload current licences, insurance, certifications and portfolio documents. My Service Photos is for examples of your own completed work.

File quality

Use clear, legible and current files. Service evidence photos for a live Job must be uploaded through that Job's lifecycle buttons.

12

Profile & Account

Maintain your display name, phone, public information and profile photo. Your profile photo can be shown to customers when you are their assigned professional.

Security

Use Change password when needed and sign out when using a shared device.

Before leaving a completed service

If something does not look right

Do not create duplicate actions repeatedly. Refresh once and review the assignment status. If the issue remains, contact PLEASE Administration and provide the Job Reference shown in the portal.

Never share your login credentials or another Provider's account.
