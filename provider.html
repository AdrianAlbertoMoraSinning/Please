PROVIDER USER MANUAL

How to use your PLEASE Provider Portal

Current guide for assignments, schedules, service evidence, rates, documents, profile information and Worker Type rules.

PLEASE PROVIDER WORKFLOW

Follow your Worker Type lifecycle

Every action creates operational history for PLEASE Administration. Independent Providers use arrival and completion evidence. PLEASE Staff must complete all four required photos in sequence.

1Review + Confirm2PLEASE Staff: Check In + Photo3I’ve Arrived + Photo4Start Service5Complete + Photo6PLEASE Staff: Check Out + Photo

Worker Type determines your photo sequence

Independent Provider: I’VE ARRIVED photo → START SERVICE → COMPLETE SERVICE photo.

PLEASE Staff: CHECK IN photo → I’VE ARRIVED photo → START SERVICE → COMPLETE SERVICE photo → CHECK OUT photo.

Your Worker Type is controlled by PLEASE Administration. Do not skip or reuse evidence photos.

01

Overview

Use Overview as your home screen. Check Pending, Confirmed and Completed counters and review your next assignments.

Good practice

Refresh the portal before starting your workday and whenever Administration tells you a Job has changed.

02

My Calendar

Your calendar displays only your own assignments. Use Previous, Today and Next to navigate months and select an event to see Job details.

Privacy

You cannot see another Provider's calendar, assignments or compensation.

03

Assignments

Review date, time, address, scope, PLEASE notes and your compensation before accepting. Confirm or decline promptly.

Admin notification

Your Confirm or Decline response is recorded immediately and PLEASE Administration is notified.

04

Schedule Changes

Use Propose Schedule Change when an assigned time must change. Date, start and end use 15-minute increments.

Important

The original schedule remains official until PLEASE Administration approves the change.

05

Worker Type

Your Account screen shows either Independent Provider or PLEASE Staff.

PLEASE Staff

PLEASE Staff has a mandatory four-photo service sequence. Independent Provider keeps the standard two-photo evidence workflow.

06

Check In — PLEASE Staff

After the Job is confirmed and within the allowed start window, choose CHECK IN and take the first official photo.

Sequence rule

PLEASE Staff cannot record I’VE ARRIVED until Check In is complete. Independent Providers do not see this step.

07

I've Arrived

At the service location choose I’VE ARRIVED and take the official arrival photo. Mobile TAKE PHOTO opens the camera and PLEASE automatically prepares the image.

Evidence rule

The photo must correspond to your own assignment. PLEASE Staff records this as photo 2; Independent Provider records it as the first required service photo.

08

Start Service

After arrival is recorded, choose START SERVICE. This changes the operational lifecycle to in progress.

Do not skip steps

Independent: CONFIRMED → ARRIVED → IN PROGRESS. PLEASE Staff: CONFIRMED → CHECKED IN → ARRIVED → IN PROGRESS.

09

Request More Time

If additional time is needed, request an extension, select the hourly item, additional minutes and reason.

Approval

Extra time is not final until the required customer/PLEASE approval workflow is completed.

10

Complete + Check Out

Choose COMPLETE SERVICE when your work is finished and take the completion photo. PLEASE Staff then has one final action in History: CHECK OUT with the fourth photo.

Multi-Provider Jobs

Every Provider completes their own lifecycle. One Provider's evidence never satisfies another Provider's requirements.

11

Availability & Hours

Maintain normal weekly working hours and date-specific exceptions using :00, :15, :30 or :45 times.

Keep it current

Accurate availability helps PLEASE prevent scheduling conflicts.

12

My Service Rates

Maintain the amounts you charge PLEASE for authorized services. PLEASE Administration can also maintain these Provider rates.

Financial separation

Provider compensation is separate from the customer selling price. Existing Job financial snapshots remain unchanged when future rates change.

13

Documents & Service Photos

Upload current licences, insurance, certifications and portfolio work. Live Job evidence must always be uploaded through the Job lifecycle buttons.

Camera quality

PLEASE uploads supported JPG/PNG/WEBP photos directly when they are already within the safe size limit, and converts/optimizes larger compatible phone images when required.

14

Profile & Account

Maintain display name, phone, public information and profile photo. Check Account for Worker Type, access status and technical history.

Security

Change your password when needed and sign out on shared devices.

Before leaving a completed service

If something does not look right

Before opening the camera, the portal checks whether that evidence step is allowed. If it is too early or another lifecycle step is required, the portal shows the exact reason and, when applicable, the time the photo becomes available. Do not repeat duplicate actions. Refresh once and review the assignment lifecycle. If the issue remains, contact PLEASE Administration and provide the Job Reference and the error code shown in brackets.

Never share your login credentials or another Provider's account.
