# CAL 1.5 Legal Agreement

The authoritative in-product agreement is published in `disclaimer.html` under **Disclaimer & Data Custody**.

Current version: `CAL-LEGAL-1.0-2026-09-05`  
Effective date: September 5, 2026

The agreement covers:

- customer ownership and custody of data;
- limited authorization for Lottus technical access;
- customer responsibilities;
- sensitive-data restrictions;
- third-party infrastructure and outages;
- cybersecurity and external events;
- backups and business continuity;
- accounting/tax and automated-processing disclaimers;
- privacy and security incidents;
- customer-controlled infrastructure;
- service suspension and force majeure;
- exclusion of indirect damages;
- limitation of liability;
- customer indemnification;
- termination and data portability;
- electronic records/signatures; and
- governing law and mandatory-law savings language.

Do not edit the production legal text without changing the agreement version and requiring re-acceptance.
